Turnout here is the vote in each state's largest race so far, divided by its citizens eligible to vote. It runs slightly below total ballots cast, since some voters skip the top race, and it will rise as counting finishes, most of all in states that vote mostly by mail (California, Washington, Oregon, Utah), where "100% of precincts reporting" still leaves ballots uncounted.
