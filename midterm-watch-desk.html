    What's on the desk. Senate and governor races that Cook Political Report, Inside Elections or Sabato's Crystal Ball rate Toss-up, Tilt or Lean, plus the 18 House seats Cook rates Toss-up. Ratings as compiled on Wikipedia on Nov 3: Cook Nov 2 (governors Oct 29), Inside Elections Oct 29, Sabato Nov 2.

    Times. Each slot is the last poll close that gates a call: a state's final close for Senate and governor, the district's own close for House seats. Closing times were cross-checked between pollcheck.co.uk and political.org. Results can lag the close by hours, and close races in mail-heavy states can take days.

    Live counts. Vote counts come from civicAPI, a free results API. A checker run from a Claude session asks it about every state on this desk every 3 minutes and writes the numbers here, so counts trail civicAPI by a few minutes and stop when that session stops. “Reporting” is civicAPI's percent_reporting, which may count precincts rather than votes. Shares are of the votes for the candidates civicAPI lists. Expected totals appear only where civicAPI publishes them. A “✓ civicAPI” mark is civicAPI's winner flag, not an AP call.

    Tallies. Before the election the Senate stood at 53 R, 45 D and 2 independents who caucus with Democrats; 51 seats is a majority. The Senate net change counts only the races on this desk and assumes every other seat stays with its party. The tallies count only the calls you mark here.
